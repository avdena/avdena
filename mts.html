Work Without Drama

Digital activation AI-generated content Valentine's Day
Year
2022
Category
Employer Branding
Award
🏆 Gold · PROBA
Challenge
MTS had updated its employer brand and needed a bold launch — something that would cut through the noise, reach young talent, and reposition the company away from its old corporate image.
Idea
We turned a universal workplace pain into a creative platform. On Valentine's Day — the most dramatic day of the year — we launched a fake video streaming service dedicated entirely to workplace dramas. Every "film" was a story about the painful, funny, and all-too-familiar moments people have with their jobs. The twist: MTS had the antidote to every drama.
Execution
The site was built around a collection of fictional films — each with a familiar title twisted into a workplace wordplay: Fantastic Projects and Where to Find Them, The Big Breakdown Theory, How to Train Your Junior. Users browsed posters and titles, read a synopsis describing a classic workplace drama — toxic management, bureaucracy, brutal schedules — then hit the antidote button, where MTS responded directly.
Then users could generate their own poster: write a creative title, craft a synopsis about their personal work drama, and enter a competition. One of the first uses of AI-generated visuals in employer branding in 2022, before the technology became mainstream. Illustrators refined the MTS-branded posters for quality.
