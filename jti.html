Challenge
JTI had updated its global corporate values — but values on a poster and values people actually live by are two different things. The task was to make 4,000+ employees across factories, offices, and logistics centres not just aware of the new framework, but genuinely engaged with it.
Idea
Instead of a traditional launch campaign, we built a year-long communication system around a custom board game — IMAJTINARIUM. Each of the four values got its own symbolic world: mirror, neon, soft, and precious. The 72 cards were created using Midjourney — based on metaphors and associations collected from employees themselves. The values literally came from the people.
Execution
The campaign ran across all internal channels — town halls, Viva Engage, offline installations, and email. At its core was a gamified platform with 20 different game mechanics for exploring the values: employees earned points, competed on a leaderboard, and worked towards becoming the top values champion.
The grand prize was the physical board game itself, along with branded company merch. To close the campaign, we produced our own unboxing video — a playful instruction on how to use the game both at work and at home. The game then became a permanent part of onboarding and team training, not a one-off activation.
