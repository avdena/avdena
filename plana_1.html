The Challenge

Designers and art directors spend their lives searching for stock photos — scrolling through watermarks, settling for generic images because custom shoots cost too much. Plan A Photoproduction is the affordable alternative.

The task: make the target audience feel the problem before selling them the solution.

The Idea

If designers are stuck looking at the world through stock watermarks — show them exactly that. Literally.

We covered the mirrors in the festival bathrooms with photostock watermarks. Everyone who looked at their reflection saw themselves as a stock photo. The message: Stuck in photostock? Use Plan A.

One mirror. Zero media budget. The whole festival as the audience.

The Execution

The Context

The campaign ran during the White Square international advertising festival in Minsk — where the entire audience was art directors, designers, and creative professionals.

The ideal moment, the ideal room, the ideal medium.

The Mechanics

Anyone who photographed themselves in the mirror and posted it to social media received a discount on a photoshoot with Plan A.

The mirrors became a popular selfie object — including among the festival's own clients. The idea spread organically, without any additional media spend.
