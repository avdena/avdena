The Challenge

The petrochemical industry has a gender perception problem. Women who choose to work in production face a wall of stereotypes — from family, from society, from the industry itself. SIBUR needed to change that narrative and signal clearly: this is a place where ambition has no gender.

The goal: build awareness that women belong in production — and make SIBUR the employer that says it first.

The Idea

It doesn't matter what others say. What matters is what you decide.

We studied the cultural context, analysed the market, and — most importantly — talked to the women of SIBUR. Their stories shaped a new Target Value Proposition: "For women who are tired of stereotypes, SIBUR is the ideal workplace — because we know ambition and passion have no gender." The slogan followed from there.

The Execution

We made a manifesto film. No scripts, no invented characters — real SIBUR employees from different divisions and cities, each with their own doubts, contrasts, and wins. Women who chose production work consciously, and prove every day that only a woman can decide where she belongs and what drives her.

In every frame we tried to capture their authenticity and inner freedom — showing that boldness and care, femininity and professionalism, strength and softness can coexist in the same person.

The Distribution

We stepped outside traditional HR channels: digital screens in fitness clubs and Post pickup points, plus transport placements across SIBUR's cities. Three very different environments — three different segments of the same audience.

SIBUR became the first employer in its category to say it directly: women have the freedom to be themselves. Not "career vs. home" — but the right to be both, or neither, or something entirely her own.
